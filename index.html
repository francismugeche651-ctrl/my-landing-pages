FAQs

Questions parents often ask

Which levels does Elyss School offer?

Elyss School offers early learning and primary education. Availability varies by class and campus, so share your child’s age in the enquiry form and the admissions team will confirm the right level.

What curriculum does the school follow?

The school follows Kenya’s Competency-Based Education (CBE) pathway. It has also announced an International Curriculum option at the Lusigetti Main Campus from January 2027 – please ask the admissions team for current details.

Where are the campuses?

Elyss School has a Foundation Campus in Thogoto (Kikuyu Road, Kikuyu) and a Main Campus in Lusigetti, both in Kiambu County. See campus details and directions.

Is Elyss a Christian school?

Yes. Elyss School is a private, Christian, child-centred school. Its motto is “Fun Learning. For a Firm Foundation. For God’s Glory.”

How much are the school fees?

Please contact the admissions team for the current fee structure and any additional charges. You can ask in the enquiry form or call the school.

Can I visit the school?

If you would like to see the school, mention it in your enquiry message or call the school, and the admissions team will advise you on the options.

Do you offer transport, meals or after-school care?

Please ask the admissions team about transport, meals, school hours and other services at your preferred campus.
